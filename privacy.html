MasteryCap Privacy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Capricorn Systems

Summary

MasteryCap is a local Progressive Web App. Progress, journal, and certificates stay on your device. No accounts, analytics SDKs, ads, or trackers ship in the app.

What stays on your device

  • Profile name and preferences
  • Course progress, flashcards, quizzes, and certificates
  • Trade journal / desk checklist entries

What leaves your device

ServiceWhatWhen
GitHub PagesStatic app assetsOn load / update
No third-party learning APIs are called by the shipped PWA.

Education notice

MasteryCap is educational. Nothing here is financial advice. Certificates are self-issued and not accredited.
